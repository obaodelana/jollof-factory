title: "Pizza with a Twist"
description: "Swap out boring marinara for a bold, African-inspired base."
image: "https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1594007654729-407eedc4be65?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80"
ingredients:
  - "1 Pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)"
  - "3-4 tbsp Jollof Factory Base"
  - "1½ cups Shredded mozzarella cheese"
  - "½ Red onion, thinly sliced"
  - "1 Bell pepper (red or yellow), thinly sliced"
  - "Your preferred protein (e.g., grilled chicken, sausage)"
  - "Sweetcorn (optional)"
  - "Fresh basil or parsley for garnish"
directions:
  - "Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). Place a pizza stone or baking sheet inside to heat up."
  - "On a lightly floured surface, roll out your pizza dough to your desired thickness."
  - "Carefully transfer the dough to parchment paper. Spread 3-4 tbsp of Jollof Factory base evenly, leaving a small border."
  - "Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce, then add your toppings: red onion, bell pepper, protein, and sweetcorn."
  - "Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned."
  - "Garnish with fresh basil or parsley, slice, and enjoy a delicious African-inspired pizza!"
