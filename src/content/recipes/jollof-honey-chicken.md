title: "Jollof Honey Chicken"
description: "Sweet, spicy, and incredibly juicy. A perfect weeknight star."
image: "/images/recipes/honey-chicken.png"
ingredients:
  - "2 tbsp Jollof Factory Base"
  - "1 lb Chicken drumsticks or wings"
  - "2 tbsp Honey"
  - "1 tsp Olive oil"
  - "1/2 Lemon (for juice)"
  - "Salt & Seasonings to taste"
  - "Fresh coriander, chopped (for garnish)"
directions:
  - "Clean chicken and pat dry thoroughly."
  - "In a bowl, combine Jollof Factory base with your favorite seasonings and salt. Mix well."
  - "Add the chicken to the bowl and coat evenly. Add a tablespoon of honey and a dash of lemon juice."
  - "Allow to marinate for at least 20 minutes, or overnight for deeper flavor."
  - "Grill, bake, or air-fry chicken until cooked through and golden brown."
  - "In a small bowl, mix the remaining honey and 1 tsp of olive oil. Drizzle over the cooked chicken."
  - "Sprinkle with chopped coriander for a fresh finish."
  - "Enjoy immediately!"
