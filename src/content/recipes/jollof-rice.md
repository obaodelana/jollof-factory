title: "Jollof Rice"
description: "The iconic West African dish, made foolproof and fast."
image: "/images/recipes/jollof-rice.png"
cookingTime: "30 - 35 min"
ingredients:
  - "1 Jar of Jollof Factory Base"
  - "3 cups Parboiled raw rice"
  - "3 cups Stock (beef, chicken, or vegetable)"
  - "Salt and seasonings to taste"
  - "1 tbsp Butter (optional)"
directions:
  - "Rinse your rice thoroughly under cold water until the water runs clear, then drain."
  - "In a medium-sized pot, combine the rinsed rice with the entire jar of Jollof Factory base. Stir to combine."
  - "Pour in the stock and add salt and your preferred seasonings to taste. Stir once more."
  - "Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover the pot tightly, and let it simmer."
  - "Cook for 25-30 minutes. Check if it needs a little more water to cook throughly. Avoid stirring too much."
  - "Once the rice is cooked, turn off the heat. Add butter if you like a more buttery taste and fluff with a fork."
  - "Serve hot and enjoy the authentic taste of Jollof!"
